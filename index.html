[ ARCHIVE ] KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently Asked
Questions

Who can participate in Farlands Hackathon? +

Farlands Hackathon is open to students, professionals, and independent creators passionate about technology and innovation. Whether you're a developer, designer, or product thinker — all skill levels are welcome. Eligibility details will be confirmed by the organizers closer to the event.

What is the squad/team size limit? +

Teams must have 2 to 4 members. The first member is the team lead; the others are team members. Solo registration is not allowed.

Is there any registration fee? +

Yes. The registration fee is ₹1,500 per team, payable via UPI after you register.

How does the transition from Vishwakarma work? +

Farlands Hackathon 2026 builds on the legacy of Vishwakarma, drawing inspiration from its architectural precision and creative spirit. Returning participants from Vishwakarma will receive priority registration and special recognition during the event.
